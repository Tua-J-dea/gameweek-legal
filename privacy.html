Privacy Policy

Gameweek · Last updated 8 October 2026

Gameweek is an assistant for Fantasy Premier League (FPL). It is made by an individual developer, Trithad Triphattanasuwan ("I"). This policy explains what the app does with information. Short version: the app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and I do not collect your personal data.

What stays on your phone

  • Your FPL team ID, language and reminder settings are stored only on your device.
  • Cached FPL data (players, fixtures, your squad) is kept on your device so the app works when FPL is slow.
  • Deadline reminders are scheduled on your device. No server sends them.

Who the app talks to

  • The official Fantasy Premier League website (fantasy.premierleague.com). The app downloads public FPL data, including the public data for the team ID you enter. Like any website, FPL receives your device's IP address when it is contacted. Its own privacy policy applies to that.
  • RevenueCat (revenuecat.com), which manages the subscription. It receives an anonymous app user ID and your App Store purchase receipt, so it can tell the app whether your subscription is active. It does not receive your name or email from the app.
  • Apple processes payments and free trials. I never see your payment details.

What I do not do

  • No account, password or email is ever requested. Gameweek never asks for your FPL login.
  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no analytics.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Children

Gameweek is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device. To change your team ID, use Settings → Change team.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: trithadt@gmail.com

Gameweek is not affiliated with the Premier League or Fantasy Premier League.

  • การแจ้งเตือนก่อนปิดรับ ตั้งเวลาในเครื่องของคุณ ไม่มีเซิร์ฟเวอร์ส่งมา

  • เว็บไซต์ทางการของ Fantasy Premier League (fantasy.premierleague.com) เพื่อดาวน์โหลดข้อมูลสาธารณะของ FPL รวมถึงข้อมูลสาธารณะของ Team ID ที่คุณกรอก เว็บ FPL จะเห็นหมายเลข IP ของเครื่องคุณเช่นเดียวกับเว็บไซต์ทั่วไป ซึ่งอยู่ภายใต้นโยบายความเป็นส่วนตัวของ FPL เอง
